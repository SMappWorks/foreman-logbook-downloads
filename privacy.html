Privacy Policy

Foreman Logbook — Industrial Coating & Dry Abrasive Blasting Field Record

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Foreman Logbook is published by SM AppWorks. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

No data is collected

Foreman Logbook does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The app does not use an advertising ID.

Where your records are stored

Everything you type into the app — shift entries, site and job details, safety and weather notes, crew names and roles, QC readings, shift notes, signatures and your contact list — is saved in the app's own private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. The developer has no access to it and cannot see, recover or restore it.

Things you choose to send

  • Printing. When you print an entry, the app hands a PDF to your device's printing service. Where it goes from there is governed by that printer or service, not by this app.
  • Email. When you email an entry or your contact list, the app opens your own email app with the text prepared. Nothing is sent until you send it, and it goes through your email provider.
  • Phone calls. Tapping CALL opens your device's dialler with the number ready. The app does not place calls on its own and keeps no call records.
  • Backups. Exporting a backup writes a file to wherever you choose to save or share it. That file is then yours to look after.

Permissions

The app declares the ability to open your dialler, your email app, your printing service and the system file picker. These act only when you tap the matching button. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or photos.

Children

Foreman Logbook is a workplace recordkeeping tool intended for adults working in industrial coating and blasting. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its stored records from your device. Because nothing is held on any server, there is nothing else to delete and no request to make.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date at the top.
